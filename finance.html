What happens next?

We review your application and contact you within 24 hours with your available options and terms. No obligation until you agree.

Soft check only

The credit check carried out is a soft search — it is not visible to other lenders and will not affect your credit score in any way.

Secure & private

Your data is handled securely and only used to process your finance application. We do not share your details with third parties.

Important information

Finance is subject to status and credit approval. TattLabStudios acts as a credit broker, not a lender. Payment plans are provided via an FCA-authorised lender. Klarna services are provided by Klarna Bank AB. A soft credit check is performed during the application process — this does not affect your credit score.
