Getting here

How to reach us

TattLabStudios is at 171 Chase Side, in the heart of Enfield and easy to reach from across North London, including Edmonton, Palmers Green and Barnet. You don't need an appointment for a consultation: pop in Monday to Saturday between 11am and 7pm, have a look around and talk your idea through with the team.

Overground / Rail

Enfield Town station (Greater Anglia) is a short walk away. Trains from Liverpool Street take approximately 35 minutes.

Bus

Multiple bus routes serve Enfield Town centre. Routes 191, 307, and W8 stop nearby.

Car

Easy access via the A10. Parking available on adjacent streets and nearby public car parks.

Parking nearby

Street parking available close to the studio. Enfield Town station is within walking distance.
